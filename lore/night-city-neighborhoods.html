Night City Neighborhoods

Night City, Free State of Northern California, 2076–2077. Six districts inside the city limits, each with its own sub-districts, and the Badlands all around. Corps own the center and the sky; gangs hold most of the rest. See also Gangs of Night City and Corporations of Night City.

Watson

The old industrial north, half-abandoned since the corps pulled out after the Unification War. Cheap, crowded, dangerous, and where most edgerunners start.

Northside

Derelict docks and factories. Maelstrom territory, with the All Foods plant and the Totentanz club. Scavenger hideouts in the dead buildings.

Kabuki

The market district: night markets, ripperdocs, braindance dens, back-alley editors and black-market recorders. Lizzie's Bar (the Moxes) and a Tyger Claws presence. The BD underground lives here.

Little China

Dense residential blocks and the megabuildings — Megabuilding H10, where V starts. Tyger Claw and Maelstrom edges meet here.

Arasaka Waterfront

The corporate port on Watson's southern shore: Arasaka's docks, warehouses and a heavy Arasaka security presence. The one piece of Watson the corps kept.

Westbrook

The wealthy east. Corporate living, nightlife, and the richest neighborhoods in the city.

Japantown

Neon, pachinko, hotels, the Jig-Jig Street red-light strip and the cloud-high clubs. Tyger Claws run it, including the braindance dens.

Charter Hill

Upscale residential — gated condos, corporate families, private security that answers to the corps rather than the NCPD.

North Oaks

The old-money hills: walled estates, celebrities, corporate executives. Almost no street presence; everything is private.

City Center

The corporate heart. Glass towers, Arasaka and Militech headquarters, and the most police per block in the city.

Downtown

The commercial core — Night City Center for Behavioral Health, the hotels, the big screens, the Afterlife's richer clients. Crowded by day, patrolled by night.

Corpo Plaza

Arasaka Tower, Militech Tower, Memorial Park, the corporate plaza itself. Checkpoints, drones, and nowhere to hide.

Heywood

The largest district, mostly Latino, from gleaming corporate-adjacent blocks in the north to the barrios in the south. Valentinos territory.

Wellsprings

Heywood's prosperous waterfront: nicer apartments, boardwalks, corporate middle management.

The Glen

The civic center — City Hall, the NCPD precinct, and the Valentinos' stronghold in the same blocks. El Coyote Cojo bar.

Vista del Rey

The south-eastern barrio: Valentino and 6th Street turf, warehouses and tenements.

Santo Domingo

The industrial south-east: power plants, factories, and the housing that serves them. 6th Street territory.

Arroyo

Heavy industry — the Arasaka Industrial Park, the Petrochem power plant, the warehouses.

Rancho Coronado

The planned suburb that went wrong: identical houses, 6th Street block parties, a working-class holdout.

Pacifica

The resort district that was never finished. When the money left after the Unification War, the half-built hotels and the Grand Imperial Mall became the city's abandoned zone. The NCPD does not go in.

Coastview

The mall, the Batty's Hotel, the chapel — Voodoo Boys ground, with the Animals holding the mall.

West Wind Estate

The apartment blocks along the coast where Pacifica's remaining residents live, Voodoo Boy territory to the shoreline.

Dogtown

The walled-off southern end of Pacifica, run by Kurt Hansen's Barghest militia as a city within the city, outside NCPD jurisdiction entirely.

The Badlands

Everything outside the city limits: desert, dead farmland, solar fields, the oil fields and the wind farms. Nomad country — the Aldecaldos and the Wraiths.

North Badlands

The road north, Rocky Ridge and the abandoned town, the wind farms. Aldecaldo camp sites.

Eastern Badlands

The solar power plant, the oil fields, the Biotechnica protein farms, and the highway out.

Southern Badlands

Jackson Plains, the Red Peaks, the border crossing, the drive-in. Wraith territory.

Off-world

Orbital Air's shuttles are the only way up, and the way up is the one dream Night City sells that nobody on the street can afford.

Crystal Palace

The orbital station: a luxury resort, casino and corporate retreat in low orbit, Orbital Air's showpiece. Where the people who own the towers go to look down on them.

Tycho

The lunar colony — Tycho Colony, the mining and research settlement on the Moon. Cold, airless, corporate-run and far enough away that Night City's problems take days to arrive. The closest thing to leaving.
