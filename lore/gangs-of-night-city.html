Gangs of Night City

Gangs run everything the corps don't bother with. Most hold a district; a few hold a trade. Where they live: Night City Neighborhoods.

Maelstrom

Watson, Northside. Chromed to the point of cyberpsychosis and proud of it — red optics, bolted faces, a cult of the machine. Muscle for hire, drug and cyberware trade, and the Totentanz. Militech sells to them when it suits.

Tyger Claws

Westbrook — Japantown — and into Kabuki. Yakuza-descended, katanas and Kusanagi bikes, with Arasaka's quiet patronage. They run the clubs, the braindance dens, protection, and the sex trade on Jig-Jig Street.

Valentinos

Heywood. The largest gang in the city, Latino, Catholic, family — tattoos, gold, lowriders and a code of honor they actually keep. Protection, smuggling, and a lot of ordinary neighbors.

6th Street

Santo Domingo and Vista del Rey. Veterans of the Unification War who set up as a militia to protect their blocks, now a gang with flags and block parties. Guns, patriotism, grudges against the corps and the Valentinos.

Animals

Pacifica, the Grand Imperial Mall. Bodybuilders on a hormone and animal-supplement cocktail, not chrome — brute strength, fight clubs, muscle work for whoever pays.

Voodoo Boys

Pacifica, Coastview and West Wind. Haitian netrunners who answer to no one and go where no one else dares: past the Blackwall, into the old net. They trade in the data no one else can reach. Legends about what they talk to on the other side are mostly true.

Scavengers

Watson, no fixed turf. Organ and cyberware harvesters — they take people apart for parts and sell them to rippers. Not a gang so much as an infestation. Everybody's first job is against scavs.

Moxes

Kabuki, out of Lizzie's Bar. Formed to protect sex workers and the clubs after the Tyger Claws got too comfortable. Small, loud, protective of their own, with a braindance studio in the basement.

Wraiths

The Southern Badlands. Raider nomads who turned to piracy — hijacking convoys, stripping travelers, hitting the Aldecaldos. No code.

Aldecaldos

The Badlands. A nomad family, not a gang: convoys, a camp, a clan. Smugglers and couriers who can get anything across the border, and the closest thing to honest the outside gets.

Barghest

Dogtown. Colonel Kurt Hansen's private army — ex-Militech and NUSA soldiers who seized the walled district and run it as their own state. Arms dealers at scale.

Source · Cyberpunk 2077 and Cyberpunk RED canon (CD Projekt Red / R. Talsorian Games), summarised; not rules text
