Gear & Chrome

Gear & Chrome

Guns, chrome, rigs, and the things worth killing for.

In Night City nobody is born with an ancestry; you buy one. Every ancestry in the book has a top feature and a bottom feature, and the book already lets a mixed-ancestry character take the top of one and the bottom of another. Here that is the whole rule: pick one top-slot implant and one bottom-slot implant. That is your chrome.

The rules text is Daggerheart's, unchanged. Names and the fantasy wording — scales, wings, tusks, tongues — are still the book's until the remap renames them; the Behind the Blackwall remap page carries the flags.

Top slot first, then bottom slot, each in the book's order.
