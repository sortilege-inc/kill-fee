Communities

Your community is the neighborhood that raised you. Pick one; its feature is yours. The rules text is Daggerheart's, unchanged — only the names have moved. Where each one is: Night City Neighborhoods.

The book's community descriptions (mountains, forests, tundra) are not carried over; the neighborhood page is the description now.

North Oaks

Old money in the Westbrook hills — walled estates, private security, and a view of everything you'll never own.

Privilege: You have advantage on rolls to consort with corpos, negotiate prices, or leverage your reputation to get what you want.

Charter Hill

Westbrook's corporate families: gated condos, good schools, and a childhood spent learning which corp you belong to.

Well-Read: You have advantage on rolls that involve the history, culture, or politics of a prominent person or place.

Arasaka Waterfront

The one piece of Watson the corps kept — docks, warehouses, and a childhood inside the Arasaka compound.

Dedicated: Record three sayings or values your upbringing instilled in you. Once per rest, when you describe how you're embodying one of these principles through your current action, you can roll a d20 as your Hope Die.

Southern Badlands

The Red Peaks and Jackson Plains past the city limits, where the road ends and Wraith country begins.

Steady: You have advantage on rolls to traverse dangerous cliffs and ledges, navigate harsh environments, and use your survival knowledge.

Wellsprings

Heywood's waterfront — boardwalks, ports, and the nicer apartments the middle managers come home to.

Know the Tide: You can sense the ebb and flow of life. When you roll with Fear, place a token on your community card. You can hold a number of tokens equal to your level. Before you make an action roll, you can spend any number of these tokens to gain a +1 bonus to the roll for each token spent. At the end of each session, clear all unspent tokens.

was Seaborne

Little China

Dense Watson blocks and the megabuildings — thousands of lives stacked in one tower, most of them yours.

Low-Light Living: When you're in an area with low light or heavy shadow, you have advantage on rolls to hide, investigate, or perceive details within that area.

The Badlands

The desert outside the city — nomad convoys, dead farmland, wind farms, and the open road.

Nomadic Pack: Add a Nomadic Pack to your inventory. Once per session, you can spend a Hope to reach into this pack and pull out a mundane item that's useful to your situation. Work with the GM to figure out what item you take out.

Corpo Plaza

The towers themselves — you had a corp, a badge, and a desk, and now you have none of them.

Found Family: Once per rest, you can spend a Hope to use an ally’s Experience as if it were your own. When you do so, describe how your time with that ally prepared you for this moment.
