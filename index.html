Kill Fee

A Daggerheart Chronicle

In Night City even a cancelled job has a price.

Night City runs on reputation, and the crew had a good one — clean jobs, a fixer who vouched for them, one more gig from corp-legit. Then a job went wrong in a way no one could explain, a teammate turned up dead, and the whole city got a reason to want them that way too. This is the record of what the job really was, who paid for it, and what it cost to prove it.
