Corps & Gangs

Corps & Gangs

The corps above the skyline and the gangs that hold the streets under it.

In Night City the corps are the weather. Two of them fought a war over the city; the rest divide up the sky, the body, the net, the food and the news. Where they sit: Night City Neighborhoods.

The giants

Arasaka

Japanese. Security, banking, cyberware, weapons, and the city's biggest tower. Lost the Fourth Corporate War and came back anyway; Saburo Arasaka's dynasty runs it like a court. Patron of the Tyger Claws.

Militech

American. Weapons and private military, the NUSA's arm in the city, Arasaka's rival since the war. Sells to Maelstrom, the 6th Street and the NCPD alike.

The sky

Orbital Air

Aerospace and orbital transport — the mass drivers, the shuttles, the gate to the orbital stations and the Crystal Palace. Controls Night City's way up.

Kang Tao

Chinese. Smart weapons and aerial vehicles — the AV fleets and smart-targeting guns. The fastest-growing arms corp in the city.

Zetatech

Drones, robotics, AI and security hardware; the corp behind half the automated things that shoot at you.

The body

Trauma Team International

Medical evacuation and armed paramedics — pay the premium and an AV comes for you, guns first. Does not care who is between it and the client.

Biotechnica

Biotech, genetics, synthetic food — the protein farms in the Badlands and the pharmaceuticals the city runs on.

Kiroshi

Optics. The eyes almost everyone in the city has had replaced.

Dynalar, Raven Microcybernetics, Moore Technologies

Cyberware makers — limbs, nervous systems, implants — in the tiers below Arasaka and Militech.

The net

NetWatch

Not a corp: the agency that guards the Blackwall and hunts rogue AIs and the runners who go looking for them. Quiet, well-funded, everywhere.

Night Corp

The city's own corp — infrastructure, utilities, civic contracts, and a reputation for secrets nobody in it will discuss.

Everything else

Petrochem and SovOil

Energy. Petrochem's plant in Arroyo powers the south; SovOil is the Soviet rival.

Delamain

The AI-driven cab company — the one corp whose employee is its product.

Network 54 and WNS

The news. N54's screens are on every corner; nothing on them is an accident.

The NCPD

The police, where the corps allow them: paid by contract, outmatched by every gang, and absent from Pacifica and Dogtown entirely. MaxTac is the cyberpsycho squad.

The brands

Everything you can buy comes from a corp: Nicola, Buck-a-Slice, All Foods, Chromanticore; Quadra, Thorton, Rayfield, Archer, Makigai and Villefort on the roads; Yaiba and Kusanagi on two wheels; Constitutional Arms, Malorian, Nokota, Tsunami and Kendachi on the hip.

Gangs run everything the corps don't bother with. Most hold a district; a few hold a trade. Where they live: Night City Neighborhoods.

Maelstrom

Watson, Northside. Chromed to the point of cyberpsychosis and proud of it — red optics, bolted faces, a cult of the machine. Muscle for hire, drug and cyberware trade, and the Totentanz. Militech sells to them when it suits.

Tyger Claws

Westbrook — Japantown — and into Kabuki. Yakuza-descended, katanas and Kusanagi bikes, with Arasaka's quiet patronage. They run the clubs, the braindance dens, protection, and the sex trade on Jig-Jig Street.

Valentinos

Heywood. The largest gang in the city, Latino, Catholic, family — tattoos, gold, lowriders and a code of honor they actually keep. Protection, smuggling, and a lot of ordinary neighbors.

6th Street

Santo Domingo and Vista del Rey. Veterans of the Unification War who set up as a militia to protect their blocks, now a gang with flags and block parties. Guns, patriotism, grudges against the corps and the Valentinos.

Animals

Pacifica, the Grand Imperial Mall. Bodybuilders on a hormone and animal-supplement cocktail, not chrome — brute strength, fight clubs, muscle work for whoever pays.

Voodoo Boys

Pacifica, Coastview and West Wind. Haitian netrunners who answer to no one and go where no one else dares: past the Blackwall, into the old net. They trade in the data no one else can reach. Legends about what they talk to on the other side are mostly true.

Scavengers

Watson, no fixed turf. Organ and cyberware harvesters — they take people apart for parts and sell them to rippers. Not a gang so much as an infestation. Everybody's first job is against scavs.

Moxes

Kabuki, out of Lizzie's Bar. Formed to protect sex workers and the clubs after the Tyger Claws got too comfortable. Small, loud, protective of their own, with a braindance studio in the basement.

Wraiths

The Southern Badlands. Raider nomads who turned to piracy — hijacking convoys, stripping travelers, hitting the Aldecaldos. No code.

Aldecaldos

The Badlands. A nomad family, not a gang: convoys, a camp, a clan. Smugglers and couriers who can get anything across the border, and the closest thing to honest the outside gets.

Barghest

Dogtown. Colonel Kurt Hansen's private army — ex-Militech and NUSA soldiers who seized the walled district and run it as their own state. Arms dealers at scale.
