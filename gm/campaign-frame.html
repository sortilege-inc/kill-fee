SET All of the above.

Tone

SET The crew are professional edgerunners just short of corp-legit, with a polished reputation and a fixer who vouches for them. That reputation is exactly why they were chosen as fall guys, and it's what the frame takes from them.

Title meaning

SET "Kill fee" is the bounty on the crew, the payoff the traitor took, and a corp paying to bury the truth. Recurring motif: a fixer muttering "kill fee's gone up" as the bounty climbs each session.

NOTE GM only. The players get the title; they do not get the three readings.

Player hopes

  • Braindances: wants to dig into BD culture and tech.
  • A body that rejects cyberware: wants to explore being unchromed in a chromed world.
  • Space: likes orbital and off-world material.
  • Guns: wants to shoot things.

Each hope has a menu on Character Menus.

Leveling

SET Two level-ups (3 to 4, 4 to 5). Level 5 is the tier 3 jump (new Experience, proficiency bump, cleared marked traits), ideally landing near the climax.
