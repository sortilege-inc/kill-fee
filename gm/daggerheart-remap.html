NOTE This is a workbench, not a rulebook. Each subsystem below has a table: Daggerheart on the left, Night City on the right, and a status tag. Traits and the core loop are settled; the rest is open. Once a subsystem is SET, its Night City column is what the table pages and the character creation options get built from. Keep Daggerheart's rules text verbatim wherever a mechanic is kept — only the names change.

Ground rules

SET Daggerheart, lightly reskinned, not a full conversion.

Keep as-is: Duality dice, Hope and Fear, HP, Stress, Armor, damage thresholds. Fear reads as "the city turning on you."

Reskin freely:

  • Weapons become guns, using Daggerheart's existing ranges and damage dice.
  • Domain cards become cyberware, quickhacks, street skills, or contacts.
  • Traits take Cyberpunk RED's stat names — Movement, Body, Reflexes, Empathy, Cool, Intelligence (section 1).
  • Spellcast → Quickhack as the verb, Spellcasting → Quickhacking, everywhere (owner 2026-10-02). Spellcast Roll reads Quickhack Roll; a spell is a quickhack and casting it is initializing it. The Spellcast Trait is the Netrun Trait.
  • Magic damage → electronic damage (owner 2026-10-02).

Build bespoke: only what each character uniquely needs.

Process: players pitch a concept, match it to the closest class and subclass, rename most abilities, and hand-build only the one or two pieces that need it. Multiclassing is off; the crew runs levels 3 to 5 — Campaign Frame.

3. Ancestries (24) → Cyberware

SET Owner 2026-10-02: ancestries are not a thing in Night City; their features are cyberware. Every ancestry's two features split into a top slot and a bottom slot (the book's own Mixed Ancestry split); a character picks one top and one bottom. Cards: Cyberware, rules text verbatim.

SET Top slot renamed (owner 2026-10-02) — 23 implants; Tidekin's duplicate Amphibious dropped. Two texts changed with the name: Full Borg ("Decide what corp built you, and to what purpose"), Commlink ("your commlink array … others of your network (gang/corp)").

SET Bottom slot renamed (owner 2026-10-02) — 24 implants; both flight features kept (Flying, Flying (alt)), the teleport kept as Blur. Two texts changed with the name: Elemental Breath deals physical damage; Retract (requires Carapace) retracts "into your carapace".

OPEN Text cleanup later (owner: "some might require slight text cleanup") — the rules text is still the book's where the name moved and the words didn't: Elemental Breath ("your breath"), Hyperfocus ("drop into a trance"), Flying (alt) ("You have wings"), Tusks ("gore … with your tusks"), Ballistic Grapple ("your long tongue"), Ignite ("wreathe … in flame", "ablaze"), HUD Scanner ("hostile creatures"), Filtration System ("a small amount of water"), Mantis Blades ("scratch").

OPEN The no-chrome fixer: with ancestry = cyberware they take no slots — decide whether that's the point or whether they get a non-implant pair.
