NOTE This is a workbench, not a rulebook. Each subsystem below has a table: Daggerheart on the left, Night City on the right, and a status tag. Traits and the core loop are settled; the rest is open. Once a subsystem is SET, its Night City column is what the table pages and the character creation options get built from. Keep Daggerheart's rules text verbatim wherever a mechanic is kept — only the names change.

Ground rules

SET Daggerheart, lightly reskinned, not a full conversion.

Keep as-is: Duality dice, Hope and Fear, HP, Stress, Armor, damage thresholds. Fear reads as "the city turning on you."

Reskin freely:

  • Weapons become guns, using Daggerheart's existing ranges and damage dice.
  • Domain cards become cyberware, quickhacks, street skills, or contacts.
  • Traits take Cyberpunk RED's stat names — Movement, Body, Reflexes, Empathy, Cool, Intelligence (section 1).
  • Spellcast → Quickhack as the verb, Spellcasting → Quickhacking, everywhere (owner 2026-10-02). Spellcast Roll reads Quickhack Roll; a spell is a quickhack and casting it is initializing it. The Spellcast Trait is the Netrun Trait.
  • Magic damage → electronic damage (owner 2026-10-02).

Build bespoke: only what each character uniquely needs.

Process: players pitch a concept, match it to the closest class and subclass, rename most abilities, and hand-build only the one or two pieces that need it. Multiclassing is off; the crew runs levels 3 to 5 — Campaign Frame.
